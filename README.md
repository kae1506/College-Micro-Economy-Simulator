# 🎓 College Policy Simulator

## 🌐 Overview
The **College Policy Simulator** is an **AI-driven simulation platform** designed to explore how institutional policies shape the **well-being**, **performance**, and **behavior** of various stakeholders within a college ecosystem.  

Through this interactive system, users — such as **administrators**, **researchers**, and **students** — can adjust key policy parameters like:  
- 🕒 **Class timings**  
- 💰 **Funding allocations**  
- 🎉 **Extracurricular event frequency**  

and instantly visualize their **cascading effects** across multiple actor types in a simulated, data-informed environment.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
The simulator acts as a **sandbox for experimentation**, enabling safe exploration of “what-if” scenarios in college policy design.  
It combines the power of:  
- 🧠 **Agent-Based Modeling** — to simulate the behavior of individuals and groups  
- 🤖 **Reinforcement Learning** — to train adaptive agents that learn optimal responses  
- 💬 **LLM Reasoning** — to model nuanced real-world cause-effect relationships  

Together, these components help uncover **dynamic and ethical insights** into how policies impact the academic and social ecosystem.

---

## 💡 Vision
The long-term vision of the College Policy Simulator is to empower institutions with an **AI-assisted decision-making tool** — one that doesn’t just analyze data,  
but *understands and predicts human dynamics*, supporting more empathetic, efficient, and evidence-based governance.
