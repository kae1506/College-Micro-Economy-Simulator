The College Policy Simulator is an AI-driven simulation platform designed to model how institutional policies affect the well-being, 
performance, and behavior of various stakeholders in a college ecosystem. The simulator allows users (e.g., administrators, researchers, or students)
to adjust policy parameters, such as class timings, funding allocation, or extracurricular frequency, and observe their cascading effects across multiple 
agent types in a simulated environment.


The system’s goal is to offer a sandbox for understanding policy outcomes dynamically and ethically through agent-based modeling, reinforcement learning, 
and LLM reasoning.
